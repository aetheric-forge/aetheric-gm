# v0.3 milestone — campaign world

Version 0.3 expands the campaign workspace beyond player characters into reusable people, factions, and places the game master prepares and uses during play.

```text
Open a configured campaign
  -> find rules-supplied NPCs
  -> create or copy a campaign-owned NPC, person, or faction
  -> connect them through typed relationships
  -> place them in a nested location
  -> use that context during play
```

The milestone is complete when a game master can build a connected campaign world — NPCs, people, factions, and places — entirely within the campaign, distinguish package-sourced content from campaign-owned content throughout, and rely on stable references that survive renames, reordering, and removal.

## Planned slices

1. [Manage an NPC catalog](../stories/18-manage-npc-catalog.md)
2. [Manage people and factions](../stories/20-manage-people-and-factions.md)
3. [Organize campaign places](../stories/21-organize-campaign-places.md)

## NPC catalog boundary

The first slice combines searchable campaign-owned NPCs with read-only NPC records from the pinned ruleset. Package records can be inspected or used as sources for independent campaign NPCs. Campaign-owned copies retain their own identity and mutable state without modifying package content.

## People and factions boundary

The second slice extends the same campaign-owned identity model to people and factions that don't need rollable stats, and introduces a typed-relationship mechanism connecting any two catalog entries, including NPCs. Notes and relationships may be marked secret, kept distinct from ordinary content for a future player-facing view without introducing access control now.

## Places boundary

The third slice adds a campaign-owned place catalog with a containment hierarchy and reuses the Story 20 relationship mechanism for non-containment links such as portals or trade routes. People, factions, and NPCs gain a stable place reference for their location field.

## Release boundary

Version 0.3 ends with Story 21. Encounters, initiative, combat automation, and session-flow tooling are deliberately excluded so this release can focus on a connected, referenceable campaign world.

## Deferred beyond v0.3

- encounter composition and reusable encounter templates;
- initiative and round tracking;
- combat automation;
- maps or tactical positioning;
- procedural NPC or place generation;
- session workspace (draft/running/completed sessions, live notes, recap);
- player-facing views or access control for secret content.
