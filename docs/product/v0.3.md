# v0.3 milestone — campaign world

Version 0.3 expands the campaign workspace beyond player characters into reusable people and game-master-facing world material.

```text
Open a configured campaign
  -> find rules-supplied NPCs
  -> create or copy a campaign-owned NPC
  -> retain independent notes, state, and rules references
  -> use that NPC during play
```

The milestone begins with [Manage an NPC catalog](../stories/18-manage-npc-catalog.md). Its remaining scope will be selected after v0.2 is released and reviewed; encounters, initiative, and combat automation are not implicitly part of this release.

## First planned slice

1. [Manage an NPC catalog](../stories/18-manage-npc-catalog.md)

## NPC catalog boundary

The first slice combines searchable campaign-owned NPCs with read-only NPC records from the pinned ruleset. Package records can be inspected or used as sources for independent campaign NPCs. Campaign-owned copies retain their own identity and mutable state without modifying package content.

The NPC data shape, action vocabulary, and resource semantics must be designed explicitly during this milestone rather than inferred from the v0.2 character sheet.

## Not committed yet

- encounter composition and reusable encounter templates;
- initiative and round tracking;
- combat automation;
- maps or tactical positioning;
- procedural NPC generation.
