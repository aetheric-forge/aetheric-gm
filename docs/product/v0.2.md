# v0.2 milestone — live-play foundations

Version 0.2 begins turning the rules-enabled campaign workspace into a tool the game master can use directly at the table:

```text
Open a configured campaign
  -> roll dice manually or from a meaningful game action
  -> view and update a proper character sheet
  -> find, inspect, and use an NPC from the campaign catalog
```

The milestone is complete when a game master can open a character or NPC, initiate a correctly labelled action, understand its animated dice result, and continue working without leaving the campaign workspace.

## Planned slices

1. [Roll animated dice](../stories/16-roll-animated-dice.md)
2. Character sheet proper
3. NPC catalog

The slices should share a small vocabulary for actors, rollable actions, resources, and rules references while allowing player characters and NPCs to retain different workflows and presentation.

## Dice roller boundary

The first slice provides recognizable two-dimensional polyhedral dice, brief accessible animation, modifiers, advantage and disadvantage, contextual labels, readable totals, and recent-roll history. Results are generated before animation; animation only reveals them.

## Deferred beyond v0.2

- three-dimensional dice and simulated physics;
- a generic character-sheet designer as a replacement for a focused ruleset sheet;
- full encounter, initiative, and combat automation;
- multiplayer or player-facing character sheets;
- permanent campaign-wide roll logging;
- sophisticated migration or merge handling for evolving rules packages.
